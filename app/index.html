Research principles

Tribal nations are contemporary sovereign communities, not solely historical subjects. Use each nation's preferred name, distinguish federally recognized, state-recognized and other communities without implying that recognition defines identity, and request permission before publishing culturally restricted knowledge. No sacred site coordinates or private ceremonial details.

Starter framework only. Listings, schedules, boundaries and cultural descriptions require direct source verification before publication.
