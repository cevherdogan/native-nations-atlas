Ethics & living heritage

Center community-authored sources and preferred names. Distinguish contemporary communities from historical territories, and recognition systems from Indigenous identity. Publish only visitor-approved locations, publicly shared traditions and properly licensed cultural materials. Do not expose sacred-site coordinates or private ceremonial knowledge.

Source-led research

Each future record requires publisher, URL, date checked, community attribution, evidence status, usage permissions and a correction pathway. No inferred tribal affiliations, boundaries or cultural practices.
