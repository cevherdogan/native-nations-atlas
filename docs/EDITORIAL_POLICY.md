# Editorial and cross-reference policy

- Distinguish a nation, community, language, location, institution and event; never equate a nation with a present-day country.
- Prefer tribal/Indigenous government, community, and cultural institution sources; preserve citations, source dates and community-preferred terminology.
- Avoid exposing sacred/restricted locations, ceremonies, knowledge, or unapproved imagery.
- Label automated related-resource suggestions as taxonomy-based, not evidence of affiliation.
- Store future source status: unverified, community-authored, externally documented, reviewed; include last-reviewed timestamp.
- Contextual list suggestions: related nations, languages, lands, institutions, public events, collections, and bibliography, selected only when verified relationships exist.
- Corrections must be traceable, additive and reversible; retain prior releases and migration notes.
